Questions

Before you connect

What does Orbit access in my Google account?

Read-only access to Search Console and Google Analytics for the properties you choose. Orbit cannot change settings, users or data in either, and you can remove access at any time from your Google account.

Which AI assistants does Orbit track?

ChatGPT today, asked from India with web search on, through the official API. Each tracked question is asked on a schedule and the answer, the brands it names and the sources it cites are stored.

Do I need an SEO team to use it?

No. The overview leads with the keywords and AI questions that matter and whether they are improving; the technical detail sits below it for SEO teams. Blog posts arrive as drafts you can read, edit and publish yourself.

Does Orbit publish anything without me?

No. Orbit writes drafts and checks them, but a post goes live only when someone on your side clicks Publish. Orbit's access to your store covers blog content only.

What happens after the trial?

Your data stays in place. Talk to us to choose a plan; nothing is charged automatically.

Where is my data stored?

In a managed Postgres database in Singapore. Each brand's data is separated at the database level, so one brand can never see another's.
