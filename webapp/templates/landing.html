Questions

Before you connect

Which AI assistants does Orbit track?

ChatGPT today, asked from your market with web search on, through the official API. Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Gemini, Perplexity and DeepSeek are rolling out. Every answer, the brands it names and the sources it cites are stored.

How is the visibility score worked out?

The Hovers Visibility Score combines four things per engine: how often answers name you, where you appear among the brands named, how often your own site is cited, and how you are described. Each part is shown on its own too.

What does Orbit access in my Google account?

Read-only access to Search Console and Google Analytics for the properties you choose. Orbit cannot change settings, users or data in either, and you can remove access at any time from your Google account.

Can I track competitors?

Yes. Add the competitors you care about and every screen lets you switch between your brand and theirs: AI answers, citations, keywords and backlinks.

Does Orbit publish anything without me?

No. Orbit writes drafts and checks them, but a post goes live only when someone on your side clicks Publish. Its access to your store covers blog content only.

Where is my data stored?

In a managed Postgres database in Singapore. Each brand's data is separated at the database level, so one brand can never see another's.
