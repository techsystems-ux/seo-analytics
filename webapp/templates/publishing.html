What happens on Publish

  1. The post is created on the selected blog with its title, body, excerpt, tags and author.
  2. The meta title and meta description are set as the post's search listing.
  3. FAQs are added at the end with FAQ schema; the theme adds its own Article schema.
  4. The live link is saved in Orbit, and the post joins the site's page list for checks and internal links.

Edits after publishing stay in Orbit until someone clicks Update live post. Hide on store unpublishes it without deleting it.
